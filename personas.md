# Personas — QuickBite

Built from the 15 interview participants and segmented by ordering behavior seen
in the funnel analytics review. See [User Research](user-research.md) for the
underlying data.

---

## Busy Ben
**32, product manager, orders weekday lunches**

- **Goals:** Order the same 2–3 go-to meals with minimal thought, get an ETA he
  can actually plan a lunch break around.
- **Frustrations:** Re-navigating the same restaurant menu every day; ETAs that
  drift without warning, forcing him to keep checking the app mid-meeting.
- **Quote (interview P3):** "I know what I want before I open the app. I just
  want to not have to find it again."
- **Relevant finding:** Represents the "reordering is more effort than it should
  be" theme — Finding #2 in user research.

---

## Student Sara
**21, university student, orders late evening with roommates**

- **Goals:** Keep costs predictable, split orders easily, order quickly without
  overthinking.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't trust delivery time estimates enough to plan around
  them; often orders from whichever restaurant is fastest rather than what she
  actually wants, because ETA uncertainty makes "the good place" feel risky on a
  weeknight.
- **Quote (interview P11):** "If I don't trust the time, I just pick whatever's
  probably going to show up on time instead of what I actually want."
- **Relevant finding:** Represents Finding #1 (ETA trust) and Finding #4 (live
  tracking would raise confidence).

---

## Family Fiona
**41, working parent, orders regular family dinners 2–3x/week**

- **Goals:** Reliable, repeatable orders for the same handful of family
  favorites; minimal app interaction time given limited attention while managing
  kids.
- **Frustrations:** Reordering the "usual" family meal (same restaurant, same
  modifiers for each family member's preferences) takes noticeably longer than
  it should; when an item is out of stock, she doesn't find out until deep into
  checkout.
- **Quote (interview P9):** "By the time I've rebuilt the order I could have
  just made dinner myself."
- **Relevant finding:** Directly informed FR-03 (substitution prompt before
  checkout, not a silent drop) in the [PRD](product-requirements-document.md).

---

## How these personas shaped the decision
All three personas point at the same two levers — faster reordering and
trustworthy delivery timing — which is why both became the focus of this cycle's
[PRD](product-requirements-document.md) rather than three separate initiatives.
